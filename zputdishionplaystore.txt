Phase 3: Complete the App Setup Tasks
After creating the app, your dashboard will show "Set up your app". Click through each item:

1. Privacy Policy
Enter your privacy policy URL: https://dishi.delstarfordworks.co.ke/privacy
Click Save.
2. App Access (Crucial for Play Store Review!)
Google's review team needs to log in to test the app without being blocked.

Select: "All or some functionality is restricted"
Click + Add instructions:
Name: Student Test Account
Username / Email: (e.g. teststudent@dishi.com or your test student email)
Password / PIN: Provide the login password and security PIN.
Explanation: "Students log in via email and PIN to access campus food ordering, virtual card wallet, and meal swipes."
Click Save.
3. Ads
Select: "No, my app does not contain ads" → Save.
4. Content Rating
Start questionnaire → Enter your contact email (dishi@delstarfordworks.co.ke or support@delstarfordworks.co.ke).
Category: Utility, Productivity, Communication, or Other.
Answer the questions honestly (violence: No, offensive language: No, etc.).
Review and submit → You will receive a PEGI 3 / Everyone rating.
5. Target Audience & Content
Target age groups: Select 18 and over (university students).
Could your store listing appeal to children? Select No.
6. News & COVID-19 & Government Apps
News app: No
COVID-19 contact tracing: No
Government app: No
7. Financial Features Declaration (Important!)
Because DISHI includes M-PESA wallet top-up, peer transfers, and campus kiosks:

Check: Mobile wallet / Peer-to-peer transfers / Payments.
If asked for licensing: Select that payment processing is facilitated through authorized telecommunications partners (Safaricom M-PESA Daraja).
8. Data Safety Questionnaire
Google requires you to disclose what data is collected:

Location: Approximate & Precise Location (used for campus delivery driver tracking & nearby cafeteria kiosk search).
Personal Info: Name, Email address, Phone number, User ID (account management & verification).
Financial Info: Purchase history, M-PESA transaction IDs (for wallet balance reconciliation).
Photos and Videos: Profile photos & student ID verification.
Audio: Optional voice memo SOS / audio notes.
Is data encrypted in transit? Yes (all HTTPS/SSL to Firebase & Vercel backend).
Can users request data deletion? Yes.

Phase 5: Build the Release App Bundle (.aab)
Your android/app/build.gradle.kts is already pre-configured to sign releases with upload-keystore.jks.

To compile the production bundle, open your terminal and run:

powershell
flutter build appbundle --release
Once compilation completes, your ready-to-upload bundle will be generated at:

swapeat\build\app\outputs\bundle\release\app-release.aab
Phase 6: Upload and Submit for Review
In Play Console left sidebar, go to Release > Production (or Testing > Internal testing if you want to test on your own phone first).
Click Create new release.
Under App bundles, drag and drop: swapeat\build\app\outputs\bundle\release\app-release.aab
Enter release notes (e.g. Initial production launch of DISHI Campus Dining & Wallet v1.0.0).
Click Next > Review release.
Click Start rollout to Production (or Submit for Review).
Google will review your application within 2–5 business days and it will go live on the Google Play Store!
