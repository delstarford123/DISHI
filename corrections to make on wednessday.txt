DISHI & DeLiv Peer-to-Peer Campus
Ecosystem Enhancements
This document outlines the strategic enhancements for the DISHI and DeLiv platforms, focusing
on creating a robust peer-to-peer gig economy within the university campus. It details
role-switching capabilities, refined driver mechanics, service marketplace integrations, and
system fixes to optimize the student experience and generate localized employment.
1. DeLiv Platform Enhancements
Seamless Role Switching (Student ↔ Driver)
● In-App Toggle: Students can seamlessly switch between "Student Mode" and "Driver
Mode" via a prominent toggle switch in their profile settings.
● Unified Account, Segmented Permissions: To prevent data redundancy and enhance
user experience, students do NOT need to create a separate account. Their existing
DISHI student profile is granted "Driver Permissions" upon successful verification.
● Student Driver Identification: In the UI, student drivers are distinctly labeled as "Student
Driver" alongside their vehicle type.
● Flexible Vehicle Types: Students are not restricted to cars. They can register as drivers
using a Car, Van, TukTuk, Bicycle, or even as a "Walker" for hyper-local deliveries (e.g.,
carrying items across campus).
● Registration Safeguards: Once a student completes driver registration, the "Register as
Driver" prompt is hidden or redirects to the Driver Dashboard, preventing duplicate
applications.
● Status Overhaul: The confusing "StandBay" status is removed. Drivers are simply
"Online" (available for rides) or "Offline."
Driver Dashboard Redesign
● Earnings & Metrics: A clean, visual dashboard showing real-time earnings, completed
trips, average ratings, and passenger reviews.
● Ride Management: Clear "Accept" and "Reject" buttons for incoming ride requests, with
route previews.
● Maintenance Hub: A dedicated section where drivers upload receipts and photos for
vehicle maintenance. The system calculates these expenses and deducts them from
gross earnings for accurate net income reporting.
Rider Experience & Visibility
● Real-Time Visibility: Students seeking a ride can see nearby DeLiv vehicles (including
student walkers/cyclists) on a live map, especially those heading in the same direction or
towards campus.
● Instant Booking & Payment: Rides can be booked in real-time, with seamless payment

processing directly through the student's DISHI wallet or M-Pesa.

Escrow & Payment Infrastructure
● Budget-Friendly Escrow: Funds are locked in escrow before work begins, ensuring the
Fundi gets paid while protecting the client if the job is incomplete.
● Integrated Payments: All transactions are settled via the internal DISHI wallet or
integrated M-Pesa APIs, minimizing cash handling.

3. System Fixes & Administrative Updates
Admin Visibility
● Unified User View: The Admin Dashboard now explicitly displays registered drivers
within the standard "Users" section, ensuring full oversight of the dual-role student
populace.
Bug Fixes
● Keja Yangu Lease Error: The "attempt to write and read at the same time" error
occurring during lease signing has been resolved by implementing proper database
transaction locks and asynchronous read/write separation.
● SmartI Data Sync: The daily focus metrics are optimized to process and display real-time
data accurately without lag.
Data Privacy
● Account Deletion: A clear, accessible option is added for users to permanently delete
their accounts and wipe their personal information from the system in compliance with
data protection standards.
