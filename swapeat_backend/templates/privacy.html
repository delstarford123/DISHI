Privacy Policy for DISHI

Operated by Delstarford Works · Last Updated: October 16, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to DISHI ("the App"), developed and operated by Delstarford Works ("we", "us", or "our"). DISHI provides smart campus dining, contactless cafeteria meal ordering, Safaricom M-PESA wallet management, student community hubs, and delivery services for university and college communities.

We are dedicated to safeguarding your personal data in strict compliance with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and Google Play Developer Program Policies. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how it is used and secured, and how you can manage or delete your data.

2. Information We Collect

To provide reliable and secure campus services, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Personal Identity & Account Data: Your name, email address, telephone number, user role (student, vendor, delivery rider, parent, fundi), student registration number/campus, and profile image.
  • Financial & Payment Transactions: Safaricom M-PESA transaction IDs, payment receipts, wallet balance top-ups, peer meal transfers, and cafeteria preorder histories. We do not store your M-PESA PIN.
  • Location Information:
    Foreground & Background Location: DISHI requests precise and approximate location permissions. For students and food recipients, location is used to show nearby cafeteria stalls and real-time delivery tracking. For active campus delivery riders, location is accessed during active order dispatch to route orders safely to the correct hall of residence.
  • Camera & Media Access: Optional camera access to scan vendor QR codes at food counters, upload food listing images, or verify student ID cards.
  • Audio Access (Microphone): Optional microphone permission strictly used if a student activates the Campus Safety SOS audio voice memo recording feature.
  • NFC & Contactless Meal Swipes: Device NFC capabilities used to read and emulate virtual student dining cards at verified kiosk POS terminals.
  • Device Information & Diagnostics: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens for real-time order alerts, device model, operating system version, and crash telemetry.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to provide, maintain, and secure the DISHI platform:

  • Process food preorders and sync live prep queues with campus cafeterias.
  • Reconcile wallet balances, M-PESA STK pushes, and peer meal transfers.
  • Provide accurate delivery routing and security tracking.
  • Send transactional alerts (e.g., "Order Ready for Pickup" or "Wallet Credited").
  • Detect and prevent wash trading, balance tampering, and fraudulent transactions through automated velocity monitoring.
  • Coordinate emergency responses when a user triggers campus safety SOS alerts.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner only with industry-standard, secure infrastructure providers who adhere to strict data protection standards:

  • Safaricom M-PESA Daraja API: Processes instant mobile money deposits, C2B paybill notifications, and vendor payouts via encrypted channels.
  • Google Cloud Platform & Firebase: Provides encrypted database hosting (Firestore), user authentication, cloud storage, and push notifications.
  • Google Maps Platform: Visualizes campus cafeterias, delivery routes, and safety check-ins.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third-party advertisers.

5. Data Security & Encryption

All communication between the DISHI application and our backend servers is strictly encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Data at rest is encrypted within Google Cloud infrastructure. Local security credentials such as user PINs are hashed using cryptographic one-way hashes and stored inside Android KeyStore / FlutterSecureStorage.

6. Your Rights & Account Deletion

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act and Google Play guidelines, you retain full rights regarding your data:

  • Access & Correction: You may review and update your profile details directly in the app under Profile → Settings.
  • Right to Erasure (Account Deletion): You have the right to request permanent deletion of your account and personal profile data at any time.
To request permanent account deletion, you can either tap Delete Account within the DISHI mobile app settings, or visit our dedicated web deletion portal:
https://dishi.delstarfordworks.co.ke/delete-account

Upon deletion, all profile credentials, auth tokens, device links, and active preorders are immediately and permanently purged. Historical payment transaction records may be retained in anonymised form solely to satisfy statutory legal and tax audit requirements under Kenyan law.
