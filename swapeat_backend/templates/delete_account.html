How to Delete Your Account In-App

You can delete your DISHI account and profile data instantly at any time from within the mobile app:

  • Open the DISHI app on your Android device.
  • Tap the Profile tab on the bottom navigation bar.
  • Scroll down and tap Account & Security Settings.
  • Tap Delete Account & Purge Data and confirm your request.

Online Deletion Request Portal

If you have uninstalled the app or are unable to log in, you can request account deletion using this official web portal. Our security team will process your request within 48 hours.
