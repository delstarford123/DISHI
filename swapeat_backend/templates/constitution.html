Four Core Entrenched Statutes

Constitutional clauses governing employment, equity shareholding, compensation, and institutional partnerships

ARTICLE VII • COMPENSATION SCHEDULE

Salaried Payments Commencing 1st January 2027

Structured monthly compensation officially starts on 1st of January, 2027, paying KES 5,000.00 – KES 10,000.00 per month across foundational operational tiers plus transaction performance bonuses. The period prior constitutes foundation volunteer incubation.

ARTICLE VIII • THE 3-WEEK DESERTION RULE

Automatic Removal for 3 Weeks of Neglect

If an employee or officer ceases responsibility and neglects duties for three (3) consecutive weeks (21 days) without written approval or force majeure, they are automatically taken out of the business under Section 44 of the Employment Act, 2007 (abandonment of duty).

ARTICLE IX • THE 1-MONTH BUYOUT RULE

Mandatory Capital Refund & Exit for Inactive Shareholders

Shareholders who cease active participation have one (1) month (30 days) from formal notice to write an official explanatory letter to the Executive Wing. Failure to do so triggers an automatic full capital refund and compulsory surrender of shares (Companies Act 2015).

ARTICLES X & XI • 90% SUPERMAJORITY THRESHOLD

90% Executive Wing Vote for Partnerships & Rules

In any event of entering into an institutional or commercial partnership, contract, or MoU, the contract must receive an affirmative vote of at least 90% of the Executive Wing's shares/seats. Any new platform rule or constitutional amendment must also be approved by a 90% supermajority.
