Six Codified Diversified Revenue Streams

Multi-channel unit economics ensuring robust profitability without predatory student markups

STREAM 1 • AGENCY BANKING

0.5% Vendor Cash-In Agency Float

Cafeteria counters double as agency banking touchpoints. When students deposit cash at vendor stalls, the vendor earns 0.5% and DISHI pools the commercial float for daily settlement clearance.

STREAM 2 • PRE-ORDER MDR

2.5% Pre-Order Merchant Discount Rate

A minimal 2.5% MDR on smart pre-orders and kiosk meal pickups, saving vendors an average of 40 minutes per queue and eliminating fake M-PESA SMS fraud.

STREAM 3 • COURIER LOGISTICS

15% DeLiv Courier Share

Comrade runners deliver food directly from cafeterias to dorm rooms. DISHI takes a 15% platform dispatch fee while students retain 85% of each delivery charge.

STREAM 4 • ESCROW MARKETPLACE

5% Comrade Fundi Artisan Escrow

Student fundi mechanics, laptop technicians, tutors, and hair stylists perform campus services with funds secured in an escrow vault until satisfactory completion.

STREAM 5 • CAMPUS TICKETING

Event Passes & Ticketing (KES 30/pass)

Web-based QR ticketing for campus bashes, culture weeks, varsity sports, and academic symposiums with instant QR validator scanners for student gatekeepers.

STREAM 6 • MEAL SOLIDARITY

1.5% Campus Harambee Meal Support

Transparent crowdfunding where alumni, parents, and sponsors fund student meal wallets. A modest 1.5% processing fee maintains the transparent ledger and audit logs.
