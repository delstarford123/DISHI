Agent Architecture

How Does the Vendor Add Float to Allow for Student Deposits?

In the DISHI Agency Banking network, vendors act as local cash-in agents. Here is the exact, secure flow explaining how vendors maintain float and process student cash deposits.

1

Vendor Float Top-Up

The vendor loads digital float (E-Float) directly onto their DISHI POS via Safaricom M-PESA STK Push or utilizes daily digital food sales revenue.

2

Student Cash Handover

A student hands physical paper cash (e.g. KSh 500) to the vendor at the counter and presents their Student QR code or NFC card.

3

Dual PIN Verification

Online students enter their 4-digit Wallet PIN or Daily Auth Token. Offline students enter their verified Offline Card PIN. No unauthorized deposits.

4

Atomic Transfer & Guaranteed Commission

The vendor's float is debited by KSh 500, the student's wallet is credited KSh 500, and the vendor instantly earns 0.5% commission (KSh 2.50).

5

Real-Time Parent Alert

The student's parent receives an automated push notification and SMS receipt: "Child deposited KSh 500 at [VendorName]. Balance: KSh 1,450."
