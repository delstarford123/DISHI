02 • Problem Analysis

The Status Quo: Campus Food Insecurity & Financial Exclusion

University students navigate high financial friction daily. Distant ATMs, predatory cash withdrawal fees, spotty campus mobile data, and long cafeteria queues force students to skip meals or spend money outside of safe campus parameters.

🔴 Financial Exclusion

Students without smartphones or internet connection are locked out of digital campus food services and wallet allowances.

🔴 Vendor Inefficiencies

Vendors waste 2+ hours daily verifying manual M-PESA SMS receipts and managing coin change shortages during lunch rush.

🔴 Student Hunger

Over 35% of Kenyan university students experience temporary food insecurity when pocket money runs low before end of semester.

🔴 Lack of Parent Assurance

Parents sending money via general mobile money lack transparency into whether funds are spent on hot meals or diverted elsewhere.

04 • Campus Youth Employment & Gigs

Creating Dignified Jobs: Food Delivery (DeLiv) & The Fundi Marketplace

Youth unemployment across Africa requires practical, homegrown interventions. African universities are bustling mini-cities where tens of thousands of meals, repairs, and skilled errands take place every day.

DISHI harnesses this density to create a thriving student gig economy. By powering on-demand food delivery and a peer-to-peer artisan directory with 100% Escrow Protection, comrades earn meaningful income between lectures while local community artisans gain dignified access to the university market.

🛵 Food Delivery by Comrades (DeLiv)

Comrades with bicycles, bodas, or on foot earn KSh 40 – 150+ per delivery drop from dining halls directly to hostel rooms, classrooms, and bedsitters with complete schedule flexibility.

🛠️ Skilled Campus & Community Fundis

Verified student and town technicians offer laptop/phone repairs, hostel barbering & styling, plumbing, electrical fixes, and academic tutoring with guaranteed escrow payouts.

🛡️ 100% Escrow Protection

Client funds are safely locked in the DISHI Escrow vault before delivery or repair commences. Upon satisfactory completion, funds release immediately with zero risk of default.

🤝 Grassroots Community Inclusion

DISHI integrates local bodabodas, mama mbogas, and town artisans into an organized, digital network—advancing Kenya's Bottom-Up Economic Transformation Agenda (BETA).

💡 How Campus Gigs & Escrow Work in Practice:

  1. Order or Gig Request: A student orders cafeteria food for dorm delivery or requests a Fundi (e.g. laptop repair, room plumbing, haircut).
  2. Atomic Escrow Lock: Client funds are locked in the DISHI Escrow vault (plus flat KSh 3 or 1.5% fee). The worker receives instant confirmation that payment is held safely.
  3. Doorstep Delivery / Skill Execution: The student courier delivers the hot meal, or the Fundi performs the repair or grooming service.
  4. Inspection & Release: The client verifies the job and taps "Release Escrow" in the DISHI app.
  5. Instant Wallet Credit: The courier or Fundi's wallet is credited instantly, available for campus dining or direct Safaricom M-PESA cashout.
05 • Agent Float Architecture

How Vendors Work as DISHI Cash-In Agents & Manage Float

To empower campus vendors as licensed DISHI Agents, we implemented an institutional agency banking model similar to Safaricom M-PESA or Equity Agency Banking.

💡 The Exact Float Lifecycle:

  1. Float Acquisition: The vendor tops up their agent digital float (E-Float) instantly through an automated M-PESA STK Push prompt on their vendor terminal or accumulates digital float through daily food sales revenues.
  2. Cash Acceptance: When an online or offline student hands physical cash (e.g. KSh 500) to the vendor, the vendor enters the amount on their POS numpad.
  3. PIN Security Check: The student enters their 4-digit Wallet PIN or Offline Card PIN on the vendor terminal. No deposit can occur without student authentication.
  4. Instant Atomic Settlement: The vendor's digital float is debited by KSh 500, the student's wallet is credited KSh 500, and the vendor's wallet is credited a 0.5% commission (KSh 2.50).
  5. Automated Parent Notification: The student's parent receives an instantaneous SMS and push receipt alert with deposit amount, agent name, and child's updated balance.
