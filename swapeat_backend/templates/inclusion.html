Universal Inclusion.
Barrier-Free Campus Dining.

Technology is only truly innovative when it empowers every member of our society. DISHI is built from the ground up to ensure comrades gifted differently enjoy complete independence, dignity, and nutrition.

👁️ AUDITORY & TACTILE ACCESSIBILITY

Blind & Visually Impaired Comrades

For visually impaired students, reading cafeteria handwritten chalkboard menus or navigating touchscreen keypads in noisy dining halls is a constant barrier. DISHI introduces acoustic guidance, screen-reader optimized layout architecture, and tactile offline NFC passes.

  • 🔊Acoustic Counter Chimes: Cafeteria pickup stations emit distinctive localized auditory tones when the student’s order is plated.
  • 🎙️Voice Navigation: Complete auditory menu exploration, budget calculation, and order confirmation in Swahili and English.
  • 💳Tactile Braille Notch Cards: Custom embossed tactile NFC cards allowing blind comrades to pay at counters in under 300ms without seeing the terminal.
🧏 VISUAL & HAPTIC SYNCHRONIZATION

Deaf & Hard-of-Hearing Comrades

Cafeteria counter staff shouting order numbers across a crowded university dining hall leaves deaf comrades waiting indefinitely while meals grow cold. DISHI eliminates acoustic dependence through synchronized haptic pulses and visual queue screens.

  • 📳Haptic Vibration Alerts: Students feel distinct rhythmic vibration patterns on their smartphones or DISHI wearable passes the exact second food is ready.
  • 📺High-Contrast Kitchen Displays: Large, color-coded visual order numbers displayed on monitors at cafeteria collection windows.
  • 🤝Non-Verbal Counter Checkouts: One-tap QR and NFC token validation requires zero verbal back-and-forth at the till.
♿ PHYSICAL BARRIER ELIMINATION

Mobility-Challenged Comrades

Steep cafeteria staircases, overcrowded corridors, and counter heights built above wheelchair eye level are structural injustices. DISHI bridges the physical divide with low-elevation terminals and doorstep delivery.

  • 🚪Priority Hostel Doorstep Delivery: Subsidized delivery by verified student couriers straight to student rooms.
  • 📏Wheelchair-Friendly POS Mounts: Accessible tap-to-eat hardware mounted at compliant low elevations for seated students.
  • ⚡Zero-Queue Priority Pass: Mobile pre-orders allow comrades to bypass crowded physical lines entirely.
