========================================================================
                      DISHI SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & FEES
========================================================================


The DISHI system is a multi-tier fintech and lifestyle ecosystem built to securely facilitate meals, transport, and networking in a closed-loop school/campus environment. It connects Students, Parents, Vendors, and Drivers via a central flutter app and a secure Python backend.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. STUDENT DASHBOARD
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Role: The consumer who buys meals, orders transport, and networks using their digital wallet.
Features:
- View real-time wallet balance and transaction history.
- Offline and Online Payments: Can generate offline QR codes or tap NFC cards at vendor terminals to pay.
- Preorders: Can order food ahead of time directly from the app.
- Okoa Food: A micro-credit facility allowing students to overdraw their wallet up to Ksh 500 if enabled.
- Harambee "Fund Me": Can share a custom web link to invite friends and family to fund their campus meals directly via an external M-PESA STK push.

Fees:
- Top-Up Fee: Ksh 1 per M-Pesa top-up (deducted before hitting the wallet).
- Purchase Fee: Ksh 0.0 (Students pay exact menu prices).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PARENT DASHBOARD
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Role: The financier who funds and monitors the student's activities.
Features:
- Student Monitoring: View all linked students' balances, real-time POS spending, and preorders.
- QR ID Cards: Parents can instantly download a ready-to-print, formatted PDF ID card for offline children.
- Funding: Top-up student wallets via seamless M-PESA STK Push.
- Savings Vault & Balances: Dedicated settings to manage the Vault, displaying real-time comparisons of the Active Wallet vs. Vault balance. Parents can securely fund the Vault directly via STK push.
- Harambee "Fund Vault": Generate a web link for external relatives to directly fund the Shared Vault without needing the app.
- Spending Controls & Security: Set strict daily spending limits, freeze accounts instantly (Kill Switch), manage dietary category blocks, and configure Medical/Allergy Safety Settings that alert vendors.
- Support Tickets: Directly accessible from the "Do more with DISHI" hub.
- Cash-Out: Parents can withdraw funds from their savings vault back to their M-PESA.

Fees:
- Wallet Top-Up Fee: Ksh 1 per M-Pesa transaction.
- Withdrawal Fee: Ksh 1 to cash out from the parent vault.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. VENDOR DASHBOARD
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Role: The merchant who fulfills food orders and collects payments.
Features:
- Point of Sale (POS): Scan student QR codes or tap NFC cards to instantly charge student wallets.
- Visual Verification: Displays the student's profile photo and name upon scanning to prevent card sharing/fraud.
- Preorder Management (KDS): Accept and fulfill food preorders placed by students.
- Analytics: Live metrics showing today's revenue, order counts, top-selling items, and historical trends.
- Staff & Operations: Manage staff shifts, waste logs, maintenance tickets, and inventory.
- Payout Hub: Withdraw earnings instantly to M-PESA Personal (B2C), Paybill (B2B), or Buy Goods Till (B2B).
- DISHI Agent (Human ATM): Vendors act as top-up agents for students. Students give physical cash to the vendor, and the vendor scans their QR to instantly transfer digital E-Float to the student's wallet.
- Float Mechanics: Vendors naturally earn E-Float by selling food. Brand new vendors can manually inject initial E-Float by depositing their own cash via M-PESA STK Push directly from the dashboard.
- Offline PIN Authorization: When a vendor tops up an offline student's wallet, the parent's generated 4-digit Offline PIN is required to authorize the deposit, preventing unauthorized top-ups.

Earnings / Commissions:
- Vendor Top-Up Commission: Vendors earn instant commission for topping up students. 
  * Under 100 KES: Flat 2 KES profit instantly.
  * 100 KES and above: 0.5% profit instantly.
  (Commission is instantly credited back to their E-Float balance).

Security & Anti-Fraud Protocols:
- 1:1 Wash Trading Ratio: A vendor cannot earn top-up commissions if their total top-up volume outpaces their legitimate food sales volume.
- Wash Trading Cooldown: If a student tops up at a vendor and buys food from that exact same vendor within 30 minutes, the food sale processes but the vendor's top-up commission is instantly revoked.
- Daily Commission Cap: Vendors are mathematically capped at earning a maximum of 500 KES in top-up commissions per day to prevent systemic draining.
- Lost Token (NFC) Protections:
  * Dynamic PIN Thresholds: Tap-and-go is allowed for small amounts. Transactions >= 200 KES force the student to enter their secure 4-digit PIN on the vendor's device.
  * Visual Verification: Vendors are forced to verify the student's profile photo on the POS screen before processing any payment.
  * Panic Button: Students can instantly freeze their NFC card via the app, blocking the backend from processing any further transactions for that token.

Fees:
- Transaction Processing Fee: Ksh 0.4 deducted automatically from the vendor's earnings on every successful sale (this is the core system revenue).
- Cash-Out / Withdrawal Fee: A flat Ksh 1 fee to withdraw any amount to their M-PESA or Till.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. DELIV (DRIVER, LOGISTICS, & MAPS)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Role: The driver network fulfilling food deliveries and student transport.
Features:
- Ride Sharing & Deliveries: Streamlined feed for Mtu wa Mkono (moving gigs) and Food delivery drops.
- Live Map Tracking: Real Google Maps integration with Geolocator allows parents and students to securely track drivers via their device location (secured via ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION permissions).
- Driver Registration & Docs: Secure driver onboarding with document uploads stored in user-partitioned Firebase Storage buckets.
- Driver Withdrawals: Drivers can instantly withdraw earnings to M-PESA Number, Paybill, or Buy Goods Till via real Python backend endpoints.
- Account Management: Drivers can toggle Online/Offline status and securely Logout.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. KEJA YANGU (STUDENT HOUSING & UTILITIES)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Role: Real estate ecosystem for students, landlords, and caretakers.
Features:
- Smart Leases & Clearances: Digital management of housing agreements and clearance forms.
- Maintenance Tickets: Students can file One-Tap Maintenance SOS tickets routed to caretakers.
- Rent & Utility Splitting: Automated cost sharing between roommates.
- Bulk WiFi: Hostels pool funds for bulk internet purchasing.
- Keja Streaks & Ratings: Gamified on-time rent streaks and caretaker ratings for ecosystem trust.
- Advanced Merchant Dashboard:
    * Generate E-Leases instantly as PDFs.
    * Move-in / Move-out photographic inspection checklists.
    * Bulk Tenant Import via CSV parsing.
    * Vacancy Marketing Blasts to nearby students.
    * Live Tenant Trust Scoring (KYC) based on payment history.
    * Dedicated WhatsApp Reminders (room-specific targeting) & In-App Broadcast messaging explicitly routing to active tenants via FCM.
    * Premium UI: Horizontal swipe-to-scroll Quick Actions grid and modern room management property listings.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. FIND YOUR MATCH (NETWORKING & DATING HUB)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Role: The social layer of DISHI for verified students.
Features:
- Student Match Network: A platform to connect with verified students.
- AI Wingman: An NLP-powered chat assistant built with Groq API to help students break the ice.
- DISHI Gold (Premium): 
    * 50 KSH / month subscription via M-Pesa STK Push.
    * Perks: See who liked you, unlimited swipes, priority placement.
    * Curated Perfect Matches via Email (Mon, Wed, Fri at 8:00 AM).
- Safe Walk & Partner Venues: 
    * Safe Walk: Location sharing and emergency SOS alerts.
    * SOS Alert Button: Triggers immediate logging in Firestore and dispatches a high-priority email to the admin.
    * Partner Venues: Dynamic UI linked to actual verified DISHI Vendors showing venue images and categories for offline meetups.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. CORE INFRASTRUCTURE & SECURITY
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Database (Firestore): 
    * Securely stores user balances, transactions, and analytics.
    * Hardened Security Rules: Write access is strictly partitioned so only document owners (e.g., student_id, landlord_id) can modify sensitive tables like `okoa_loans`, `rent_splits`, and `maintenance_tickets`.
- Storage (Firebase): Partitioned via rules ensuring users can only upload files (Driver docs, receipts) to their own `/uid/` directory.
- Authentication: Firebase Authentication handles secure login across all user roles.
- Push Notifications (FCM): Instant cross-dashboard alerts for sales, preorders, and top-ups.
- Payment Gateway (M-PESA Daraja): STK Push, B2C, and B2B integrated via Python.
- Python Backend (Vercel): 
    * Handles M-PESA callbacks asynchronously.
    * Uses HMAC cryptographic signing.
    * Houses the External Web Portal (Harambee).
    * B2C/B2B payout endpoints for Vendors and Drivers (`/api/mpesa/driver_withdraw`).
    * Handles Vercel Cron Jobs (`vercel.json`) for automated matching emails via `flask_mail`.
    * Admin endpoint integrations for Global Management and SOS dispatch.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. UNIFIED RENT ECOSYSTEM (HOUSE OWNERS, PARENTS, STUDENTS)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Role: An ecosystem to manage housing, rental payments, and risk management between landlords and student guardians.
Features:
- Predictive Default Analytics: Flags high-risk tenants using AI based on past rent delays.
- Keja Protection (Insurance): Landlord-opted property protection.

Fees & Commission Structure (Rent Payments):
- DISHI charges a fixed commission (e.g. KES 50) per rent transaction.
- If the Landlord opts into "Keja Protection", a fixed insurance premium (e.g. KES 500) is applied.
- The Parent/Guardian bears the full cost of the Commission and Insurance Premium on top of the Base Rent. 
- Example breakdown for a KES 5000 rent payment: 
    * Rent = 5000
    * DISHI Commission = 50
    * Insurance = 500 (if enabled)
    * Parent pays via STK Push: KES 5550 Total
    * Landlord receives in wallet: KES 5000 (Base Rent)
    * DISHI Admin Pool receives: KES 550 (Commission + Insurance)

9. SmartI (ACADEMIC STUDY COMPANION)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Role: A comprehensive academic planner and AI-driven study companion for students.
Features:
- Academic Profile Setup: Students configure Academic Year, Semester, and Units (with CAT/Exam targets). Features a seamless navigation flow allowing students to easily jump to Daily Focus, Flashcards, or the Global Leaderboard directly from Setup.
- Interactive 2D Visual Timetable Builder: A scrollable grid (Days vs Hours) that students can dynamically customize, color-code by task type, automatically generate via AI, and save persistently. Incorporates fixed commitments, subject difficulty matching, and break buffers.
- PDF Printing: Allows students to generate and print a physical PDF of their weekly study plan.
- Results Logging & Analytics: Students securely log actual achieved scores. The interactive dashboard features dynamic charts (Bar, Line, Area, Pie) allowing students to visually compare Targets vs Actuals for each unit.
- AI Study Advice (Groq NLP): Analyzes the student's actual performance against their targets via the Groq LLM API. The Python backend generates real-time, actionable study advice.
- Pomodoro Focus Timer: Customizable focus sessions (e.g. 15/25 mins) with Voice Reminders (TTS) announcing halfway/warning marks.
- Gamification & Streaks: Tracks daily consecutive focus days. Breaking a streak resets it to zero.
- Spaced Repetition Flashcards (SM-2): AI-powered interval scheduling to enforce memory retention over time.
- Backward Planning (Assignments): Smart countdowns parsing due dates and splitting them into daily micro-chapters.
- Productivity Heatmaps: GitHub-style contribution grid reflecting daily focus intensity.
- Social Leaderboard: Global ranking system based on Total Focus Hours. Top 3 students receive Gold/Silver/Bronze highlights. Supports Alias / Anonymous modes.
- Strict Mode (App Trap): Leverages `WidgetsBindingObserver` to track the app lifecycle. If the student leaves the app while studying, the timer fails, triggering a penalty and red failure screen.

Architecture Details:
- Frontend (Flutter): Integrated into the Student Dashboard's "Quick Actions" and "Do More with Dishi" hubs.
- Offline-First Architecture (Zero-Dependency): Uses `SmartimerCacheService` (via `path_provider`) for instant, offline local cache loads. An `OfflineSyncService` (via `connectivity_plus`) manages a local `offline_queue.json` to silently intercept failed network requests and background-sync them to the backend the moment Wi-Fi is restored.
- API Versioning (v3): Implemented under the `v3` namespace in the Python Flask backend.
- Database: Targets, Results, Flashcards, and Global Stats are stored permanently in Firebase Firestore under the user's `academic_profile`, `academic_results`, and `global_academic_stats` collections.

10. REGULATORY COMPLIANCE, FRAUD SECURITY & OFFLINE RESILIENCE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Role: Safeguards the ecosystem against financial fraud, ensures CBK compliance, and maintains operational continuity during network outages.

Features & Architecture:
- V4 Regulatory Compliance (CBK):
    * Hard 3,000 KES Wallet Ceiling enforced on the backend to maintain "Closed-Loop / Gift Card" exemption status.
    * Strict "No Cash-Out" policy for students.
- V4 Fraud Protection (Wash Trading):
    * 1:1 Commission-to-Top-Up velocity ratio limits on the vendor side.
    * Client-side anomaly detection paired with a dedicated Admin Fraud Dashboard to monitor vendor exploitation of e-float.
- Tiered Payment Security:
    * POS transactions over 200 KES strictly require the student to enter their 4-digit PIN.
- Offline POS Resilience (Drift SQLite):
    * The Vendor POS implements an offline-first architecture using a Drift SQLite local database (`offline_database.dart`).
    * A background `SyncService` listens to connectivity changes and pushes queued offline transactions to the backend automatically when Wi-Fi/data is restored.
- Hybrid Authentication Split:
    * Offline Students (No Phones): Parents generate a physical printed QR code PDF containing a secure 4-digit `offlinePin`. This PIN is strictly required for Vendor Top-Ups but bypassed for rapid lunch purchases.
    * Online Students (With Phones): The app leverages a `SecurityEngine` to generate cryptographic Daily Auth Tokens (HMAC SHA-256 + Time-based modulo arithmetic). These tokens are mathematically validated directly on the Python backend for Top-Ups, and evaluated in the QR payload for POS transactions.

11. DATA PRIVACY & ACCOUNT DELETION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Role: Ensure compliance with institutional data retention policies (e.g. .ac.ke domains).
Features & Architecture:
- Account Deletion API (`/security/<user_id>/delete_account`) safely disables user profiles.
- Soft Deletion: Student accounts matching `.ac.ke` domains are soft-deleted. The account is frozen and flagged for permanent deletion after 30 days in the `deleted_users` audit collection. Firebase Auth accounts are disabled rather than deleted immediately to preserve data trails.
- Hard Deletion: Non-student accounts are hard-deleted instantly.
- Data Consent Dashboard: Allows users to view their privacy policy and initiate the deletion workflow directly from their profile menu in the DashiAppBar.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
12. FUNDI JUAJI & SERVICES MARKETPLACE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Role: A peer-to-peer campus services marketplace connecting students with skilled peers (Fundis).
Features & Architecture:
- Unified Booking: Students, Drivers, and House Owners can instantly request services from verified Fundis through the "Fundi Juaji" ecosystem.
- Atomic Escrow Payments: Powered by Firestore Transactions in the Python Backend (`/api/fundi/pay`). The system safely holds the payer's money in escrow until the job is completed.
- Automated Ecosystem Tax: The platform automatically deducts a 1.5% fee from the Fundi's payout upon successful job completion.
- Fundi Dashboard: Provides Fundis with job management, chat integrations, and real-time earnings tracking.
