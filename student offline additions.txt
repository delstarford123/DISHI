# 20 Professional Features for Offline Students

To elevate the experience for offline primary and secondary school students, we need to bridge the gap between their physical lack of a device and the parent's desire for control, security, and insight. 

Here are 20 highly professional, innovative features designed specifically for offline students (using QR Codes or NFC Tags) and their parents:

## Security & Access Control
1. **Physical NFC Smart Cards/Wristbands**: Transition from paper QR codes to durable, branded DISHI NFC wristbands or keychains that are waterproof and hard to lose.
2. **Instant "Void & Reissue"**: A one-tap button for parents to instantly kill a lost tag and generate a new QR code that can be printed by the school administration, rendering the old tag useless.
3. **Restricted Time Windows**: Parents can lock the tag to only work during official school break hours (e.g., 10:00 AM - 10:30 AM, 1:00 PM - 2:00 PM) to prevent students from skipping class to buy snacks.
4. **Vendor Whitelisting**: Lock the tag so it can *only* be scanned by approved school vendors (e.g., the cafeteria, the stationery shop) and rejects transactions at outside street kiosks.
5. **Dynamic Temporary PINs**: If a child forgets their 4-digit PIN, the parent can generate a temporary, 5-minute override PIN and SMS it directly to the school matron or the vendor.
6. **Biometric Fallback**: Partner with school vendors to allow fingerprint enrollment. If a child forgets their physical tag, they can scan their finger at the vendor POS to access their parent-funded wallet.

## Health & Nutrition
7. **Nutritional Gatekeeping (Allergen Blocks)**: Parents configure allergies (e.g., peanuts, dairy) on their dashboard. If a vendor scans the student's tag for an item flagged with those allergens, the POS hard-blocks the sale.
8. **Digital "Lunchbox" Pre-orders**: Parents pre-select and pre-pay for the exact meals for the entire week. The child simply taps their tag at lunch, and the POS tells the vendor exactly what meal to hand them—no choices, no math.
9. **Category/Sugar Limits**: Beyond monetary limits, parents can restrict specific food categories (e.g., "Maximum 1 sugary drink per day"). The vendor's POS will reject the second soda.
10. **Nutritional Report Cards**: At the end of the month, parents receive a beautifully charted report (e.g., 40% carbs, 30% protein, 30% sugar) showing exactly what their child has been eating based on POS SKU data.

## Financial Management
11. **Auto-Replenish (Smart Top-up)**: The parent's app automatically deducts from the parent's Vault to top up the child's offline tag the moment it drops below a critical threshold (e.g., Ksh 50), ensuring they never go hungry.
12. **Shared Family Sibling Wallets**: Instead of managing 3 separate balances, parents can pool funds into a single "School Wallet." All 3 kids' tags draw from this unified pool.
13. **Emergency "SOS" Buffer**: A hidden emergency balance (e.g., Ksh 100) that can only be unlocked if the vendor taps an "SOS Request" button, which sends an immediate USSD/Push prompt to the parent for approval.
14. **Digital Pocket Money (Bus Fare Cash-Out)**: Parents can allocate a small portion of the tag as "Cash-Out Eligible." The student can tap their tag at the school bursar or a trusted agent to get physical cash for transport.
15. **Tag "Buddy" Transfers**: A toggleable setting that allows a child to "buy lunch for a friend" by tapping their card twice, hard-capped by the parent (e.g., only 1 extra meal allowed per week).
16. **Gamified "Keep the Change" Savings**: Parents can show the child their dashboard at home: "If you spend less than your Ksh 200 limit, the rest goes to your savings." The unspent lunch money automatically routes to a savings goal for a toy or game.

## Ecosystem & Engagement
17. **Real-Time Safety Pings (Attendance)**: When the child taps their card for breakfast or lunch, the parent receives a push notification confirming the child is safely on campus and eating.
18. **Good Behavior "Treat Bounties"**: Parents can assign offline chores (e.g., "Finish homework"). When the parent marks it done in the app, the child's tag is automatically credited with a "Treat Token" redeemable for ice cream at the canteen.
19. **Automated Transport Boarding**: Expand the tag's utility so it can be tapped on the school bus driver's terminal to verify boarding, automatically charging the fare and alerting the parent.
20. **School Admin "Oversight" Portal**: A specialized web dashboard for the school administration to view bulk data on student eating habits, manage lost cards, and easily identify students who might be skipping meals.
